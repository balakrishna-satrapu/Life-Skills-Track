# Learning Process

## 1. How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

### Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique?

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning by explaining a concept in simple words, as if we are teaching someone who is new to that topic.

If I find it difficult to explain something, it means I haven't understood it completely and need to revisit it. This helps me identify gaps in my understanding instead of just memorizing things.

When I work on programming problems, I sometimes keep trying the same approaches without making progress. The video made me realize that stepping away for some time and returning with a fresh mind can help me see things differently.

### Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

#### Active/Focused Mode

Focused mode is when we concentrate deeply on a particular task or problem.

For example, when I am learning `HashMap` in Java, I focus on understanding how keys and values are stored and how hashing works.

#### Diffused Mode

Diffused mode is when our mind is relaxed and not directly concentrating on a problem. During this time, our brain can connect different ideas.

For example, taking a walk after struggling with a programming problem might help me come up with a different approach.

**In simple words:** Focused mode helps us understand things deeply, while diffused mode helps us look at things from a different perspective.

## 3. Learn Anything in 20 Hours

### Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. **Deconstruct the skill** – Divide the topic into smaller parts to make learning easier.
2. **Learn enough to self-correct** – Understand the basics so that you can identify and correct your mistakes.
3. **Remove barriers to practice** – Avoid distractions and create time for focused learning.
4. **Practice the important parts** – Spend more time implementing and practicing rather than only watching tutorials.

## 4. Learning Principles in the Bootcamp

### Question 5: What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

Going forward, I want to improve the way I learn by following these practices:

1. **Understand before moving forward:** Focus on understanding why a concept is needed instead of just memorizing it.

2. **Explain what I learn:** Try explaining topics in my own words to check my understanding.

3. **Practice regularly:** Write code, experiment with different approaches, and learn through debugging.

4. **Break topics into smaller parts:** Divide large topics into manageable sections and learn them one by one.

5. **Avoid distractions:** Focus on one task at a time instead of multitasking.

6. **Take breaks:** When I get stuck on a problem, step away for some time and return with a fresh mind.

7. **Ask questions:** Develop the habit of asking why and how something works rather than blindly accepting it.

8. **Stay consistent:** Spend time learning and practicing regularly instead of rushing only before deadlines.

9. **Ask for help when needed:** Make a genuine attempt first and clearly communicate where I am stuck.

10. **Focus on application:** Measure my learning by whether I can explain a concept and implement it independently.

## My Main Takeaway

The biggest thing I learned is that completing topics quickly doesn't necessarily mean we have learned them. What matters is how well we understand and apply what we study.

I want to focus more on understanding concepts, practicing regularly, and learning from my mistakes. I also realized that taking breaks and asking questions are important parts of learning.

**My goal is to become an independent learner who can understand a concept, implement it, and explain it confidently rather than just memorizing information.**
