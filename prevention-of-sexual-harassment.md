# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature that makes someone feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or embarrassed at the workplace. It is not limited to physical contact.

Some examples include:

- Unwanted touching or physical contact.
- Sexual jokes, comments, gestures, or inappropriate conversations.
- Repeatedly asking someone for dates or making unwanted personal comments.
- Sending or sharing inappropriate sexual messages, pictures, or videos.
- Making unwanted sexual advances or asking for sexual favours.
- Linking promotions or work opportunities to sexual favours.
- Repeated behaviour that creates an uncomfortable or hostile work environment.

Even if someone claims it was a joke, unwanted behaviour that makes another person uncomfortable should be taken seriously.

## 2. What would you do if you faced or witnessed such behaviour?

If I faced such an incident, I would clearly communicate that the behaviour is unacceptable, provided I felt safe doing so. I would also keep a record of the incident, including dates, messages, or emails.

I would report it to the appropriate workplace authority, such as the Internal Committee (IC). If I witnessed it happening to someone else, I would support the person, encourage them to report it, and share what I personally witnessed through the proper channels if necessary.

Every employee deserves a safe and respectful working environment, and such incidents should never be ignored.
